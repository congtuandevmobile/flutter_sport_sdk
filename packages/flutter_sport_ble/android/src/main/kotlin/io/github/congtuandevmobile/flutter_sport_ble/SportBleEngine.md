# SportBleEngine.kt

**Path:** `android/.../SportBleEngine.kt` · **Layer:** native engine

Owns the devices and the shared `SensorHub` under one lock. A device goes CONNECTING, DISCOVERING, SUBSCRIBING, then READY (at least one workout measurement subscribed, a partial failure said in `error` as `PARTIAL:`) or NOT_SUPPORTED (the link works but no workout measurement could be subscribed: the connect call fails with `NOT_SUPPORTED` and the useless link is closed). Every connect gets a new epoch; `connect` fails after 20 s with `TIMEOUT`; a second connect while connecting is refused, one while READY succeeds at once. What to scan, subscribe to, read and forward raw all come from the `ProtocolRegistry`. `onNotification` takes the receipt time on arrival and feeds the hub only while SUBSCRIBING or READY, so a late packet of an older link during a new attempt is dropped. The snapshot (resolved metrics; per device: state, services, subscribed characteristics, battery, device information, R-R) is published about once per second and on every change. It never reconnects and never touches GPS. `SportBleSink` is what the plugin implements.
